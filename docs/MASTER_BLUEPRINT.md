# AXIOM Atlas — MASTER BLUEPRINT
**Atlas of eXperimental Integrity, Outcome, and Mechanism**  
Russell Labs / Biomedical Certainty Catalog  
Founded session: 2026-09-05  
Status: Living v0.1 — architecture + seed corpus + harvest pipes

## Mission
Catalogue biochemical and biomedical *findings* from the top ~100 U.S. research universities and academic medical centers. Rank each finding on a 10-level spectrum from irrevocable cause→effect to speculation. The unit of record is the **finding**, not the paper, not the lab, not the brand.

This is not a journal club. It is a causal lattice.

## The Spectrum (user direction: 1 = definitive, 10 = speculative)

| L | Name | What must be true |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Irrevocable causal proof | Independent methods + intervention + mechanism + predictive success. Revision would break large parts of biology. |
| 2 | Overwhelming confirmation | Changing the factor predictably changes the outcome across settings. Residual scope questions only. |
| 3 | Strong translational causality | Human intervention or natural experiment shows a clinically important effect. Mechanism or generalizability still open. |
| 4 | Well-established causal candidate | Convergent experiments and observations; definitive human boundary tests incomplete. |
| 5 | Robust replicated association | Consistent, biologically credible; causal intervention evidence limited or imperfect. |
| 6 | Promising causal signal | Multiple studies point the same way; replication, confounding, or effect size still materially uncertain. |
| 7 | Plausible preliminary finding | Compatible with a real effect; too thin or inconsistent for firm inference. |
| 8 | Weak exploratory association | Relationship possible; bias, chance, or reverse causation, or non-replication highly plausible. |
| 9 | Unsubstantiated claim | Little reliable support, major contradiction, or failed reproducibility. |
| 10 | Speculative or discredited | Conjecture, irreproducible headline, or subsequent contradiction / retraction. |

Grade the **finding**, not the journal impact factor. A famous paper can sit at 8. An unglamorous replacement trial can sit at 1.

## Ranking inputs (not prestige)
- Intervention test: did changing X change Y?
- Independent replication (lab + population + method)
- Mechanistic coherence (not storytelling)
- Bradford Hill + triangulation (RCT, genetics/MR, dose-response, temporality, specificity)
- Reproducibility risk (sample size, p-hacking surface, flexibility, conflicts)
- Translation gap (mouse → human, surrogate → hard endpoint)
- Stability after attempted falsification

## University set
Composite of NIH institutional funding FY2024–FY2025 (Blue Ridge / Forbes), Nature Index biological sciences 2025 (USA), and AAMC research medical schools.

Institutional repositories are first-class sources. Primary pipes (legal, open): PubMed/PMC, NIH RePORTER, ClinicalTrials.gov, bioRxiv/medRxiv, OpenAlex + Unpaywall, OpenDOAR / university IRs.

No paywall scraping.

v0.1 is the keel. The ship is supposed to get heavier.
